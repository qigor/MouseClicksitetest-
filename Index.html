How to Use This Mouse Test

This comprehensive online mouse test allows you to verify that all the buttons and sensors on your mouse are functioning correctly. Whether you're a gamer needing high precision, or just fixing a double-clicking issue, our tool covers it all.

Testing Left, Right, and Middle Clicks

Simply click inside the interactive test area above. The corresponding button on the virtual mouse will light up. The system also tracks your clicks per second (CPS) and any potential double-clicking faults.

Detecting a Double Click Issue

Double-clicking is a common hardware fault where a single click registers as two rapid clicks. Our system measures the time between consecutive clicks on the same button. If the interval is under 80ms, it is flagged as a potential double click.

Polling Rate (Hz) Explained

The polling rate indicates how often your mouse reports its position to the computer. A higher polling rate (e.g., 1000Hz) means less input lag, which is crucial for competitive gaming.
